Neighborhoods inside Coronado

Across the island

Coronado addresses are in ZIP 92118, and our office is at 836 Orange Ave. The island has a population of 21,094, and it is home to Naval Air Station North Island, Naval Amphibious Base Coronado and Sharp Coronado Hospital. For a plumbing call, the point of all that is simple. Tell the dispatcher your exact street and unit so the plumber is not searching for a gate or a building entrance.

Parts of Coronado are low-lying near the bay. The lowest drain in a house tends to be the first to back up, so if one fixture overflows while the rest are quiet, stop using water and say which one it was.

Coronado Cays

The Cays is a waterfront community on the south side of Coronado. Its sewage joins the city system, which runs 17 pump stations and more than 45 miles of sewer pipe across the island, so a backup in a Cays home may be on your lateral or on the public side. A plumber can look at the line first, and you can report a suspected public problem to the City of Coronado through its online work order system.

Cal Am notes that many leaks are not noticeable but still add to unexpected water use. If your bill jumps and nothing is visibly wet, ask for leak detection before anyone starts digging.

Country Club area and Glorietta area

The Country Club area sits in the northwest of Coronado. In older homes anywhere on the island, repeated clogs often point to an aging drain line instead of a one-off blockage, so a camera inspection can save you several rounds of snaking.

The Glorietta area is in the southeast, next to the Glorietta golf course. If you spot a soft, wet strip of ground between the house and the street, do not assume it is irrigation. Close the main valve, check whether the meter stops and call if it does.

Strand Naval Housing

Strand Naval Housing is on the south side of the island, and the Naval Amphibious Base Coronado sits along the Strand. If you live in military housing, check with your housing office about who is responsible for the pipe or fixture before you book a private plumber. For a burst line or a sewage backup, shut off the water and call us first, then tell your housing office.

Chula Vista and San Diego

Beyond the island, we serve Chula Vista, which is south-southeast across the bay by way of the Silver Strand on State Route 75 or by I-5. Its water comes from different districts than Coronado's, which changes the story for scale and pressure, so we wrote a separate page for it.

We also serve downtown and bayside San Diego, reached from Coronado over the San Diego-Coronado Bridge. San Diego's sewer rules make the owner responsible for the whole lateral to the main, which is worth knowing before you call anyone for a clog.
