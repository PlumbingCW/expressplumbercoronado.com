When every drain in the house slows down or sewage rises in the lowest tub, the main line is blocked, and you can call us at any hour because dispatch runs 24/7. Sewer line cleaning in Coronado, CA is the removal of grease, roots, paper and debris from the pipe that carries all of your household waste out to the city sewer main. Stop using water now and keep floor drains uncovered.

The first question is where the blockage sits. If it is inside your lateral, the job is yours and we clear it. If it may be in the city main, you need to report it, and we will say so after checking the line. Either way you get a clear price before work starts.

Sewage rising in a tub or floor drain right now

Wastewater coming up from the lowest drain means the main is full. Do not wait to see if it settles. We send help on same-day schedules when available, including nights and weekends, and these steps limit the damage until we arrive.

  • !Do not flush, since each flush adds to the overflow.
  • !Lift furniture legs, rugs and stored boxes out of the affected room.
  • !Look outside for a capped sewer cleanout, but do not open it unless you are prepared for sewage to rise.

Coronado local note

A system that moves 2.35 million gallons a day

The City of Coronado's wastewater division reports an average flow of 2.35 million gallons per day, including sewage from the Coronado Cays. That flow runs through city mains and pump stations before it reaches treatment, and your lateral is the last private piece of that chain.

If a backup affects a whole street or several neighbors at once, the cause may be in the city main. In that case, report it through the city's online work order system, or call City Hall at 619-522-7300. A private cleaning will not fix a public blockage.

Sewer backup, and how we trace it

A backup can start in one of three places. Debris can block the house drain, a grease or root mass can block the lateral, or something can stop flow in the city main. Which fixtures overflow first gives the first clue, because water takes the lowest exit.

We cable the line from the cleanout and see what comes back. Roots, paper wads and grease each look different on the cable. If the line opens and the backup clears, we know the cause sat in your pipe.

If the line is open and water still backs up, the problem is probably downstream. We tell you that plainly, because paying to clean a clear lateral does not help.

Cleaning the main sewer line

Main sewer cleaning uses a large cable machine for ordinary stoppages and a water jet when the line is coated or packed with roots. The cable restores flow quickly. A jet scours more of the pipe wall and is worth considering when backups have recurred.

A camera pass after cleaning shows the pipe condition. If we find sags, cracks or a broken joint, cleaning is only a short-term fix, and sewer line repair is the real answer. If the pipe is sound, you can plan periodic cleaning.

For heavy buildup, our jetting service explains how that method works and when we recommend against it.

Sewer drain cleaning for planned maintenance

Not every cleaning is an emergency. If your line runs slowly but still drains, scheduling a cleaning before it fully blocks is cheaper than an overflow cleanup. Older homes and lines with a history of roots are the usual candidates.

A planned visit lets us clean the line, inspect it on camera and tell you how soon it will need attention again. Your own routine helps too. Keep grease, wipes and paper towels out of the toilet and sink, because they are the most common cause of avoidable stoppages.

What happens when you call about a main line backup

The visit follows the same order every time so nothing gets skipped.

1

Short phone triage

The dispatcher asks which fixtures are backing up and when it began, then schedules the visit.

2

Find the access point

The plumber locates a cleanout or other access to the lateral.

3

Clear the line

A cable or jet clears the blockage, and flow is tested at several fixtures.

4

Camera check

A camera confirms the pipe condition and shows whether the cause is gone.

5

Report and advice

You get a plain explanation of what was found, including whether the City needs to be notified.

How a main line cleaning is priced

Every main line is different, so price depends on what the job needs.

Access to the line

An outdoor cleanout is quick to use. If the line must be reached through a toilet or roof vent, setup takes longer.

Length of the lateral

A longer run means more cable and more time.

Cable or jet

A cable clears most stoppages. A jet takes more time and equipment, and is quoted separately.

Camera work

A camera pass adds time but shows whether the problem is gone or damage remains.

Hour of the call

Nights, weekends and holidays can price differently, and we tell you before work starts.
