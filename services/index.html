How to pick the right service for what you are seeing

Start with where the water is and whether it is moving. Water spraying or pouring right now is a shutoff-first, call-second situation, and the page for it is burst pipe repair. A damp spot that grows slowly, a warm patch on the floor or a high bill with no obvious cause points to a hidden leak, so look at leak detection before anyone cuts into a wall.

Drains split the same way. One sink that runs slowly is usually a local clog and a drain cleaning visit. When the tub, the toilet and the floor drain all misbehave together, the problem is further down, and a camera look through sewer inspection shows what a snake cannot.

Urgent repair versus planned work

Some jobs cannot wait. A heater that is leaking onto the floor, a toilet that overflows or a drain that backs up into the house are emergencies, and the 24/7 line exists for them. Others, such as moving to a tankless water heater, replacing aging supply lines through repiping or adding a filtration system, can be booked at a time that suits you, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

Cost follows the same logic. An urgent job at 3 a.m. is priced on what failed and how hard it is to reach, and you hear the number before work starts. Planned work gives you time to ask questions, compare options and check with the City about permits. Water heater installs need a permit in Coronado, and for most other jobs, permit rules vary, so ask the Coronado Building Division.
