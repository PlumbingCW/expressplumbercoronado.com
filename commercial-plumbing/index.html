Commercial leak detection, with honest limits

A business that sees an unexplained water bill or a stained wall needs to know where the water goes. We use the same approach as in homes. The sequence is to stop fixture use, watch the meter, then narrow the search section by section with pressure tests and listening equipment.

Buildings with long pipe runs, shared walls or multiple tenants take longer to isolate, and some losses sit under slabs or inside walls that need a landlord's permission to open. We can find many leaks but not all of them, and we say so before charging for a search. Many commercial leaks also turn out to be a running toilet or a failed fixture. The method is the same one described for leak detection in homes.

Coronado local note

Restaurants and the Orange Avenue corridor

Restaurants in Coronado need a City of Coronado Food Establishment Waste Discharge permit, known as an FEWD permit, and must follow the grease-removal program. Practically, that means keeping grease out of the line and having the trap cleaned before it fills. Ask the City what your permit requires, and keep your cleaning records where an inspector can see them.

Orange Avenue is Coronado's downtown shopping, dining and theater district, and many businesses there are in leased spaces. That shapes plumbing calls in two ways. After-hours access has to be arranged with whoever holds the building keys, and the landlord often needs to approve work that touches shared lines. Call us with that contact ready.

We do not name or claim any particular business on Orange Avenue or elsewhere. Every call is a fresh dispatch, so give the dispatcher your address and building contact.

Grease trap cleaning and kitchen drain service

A grease trap catches fat before it reaches the sewer, and it only works when it is emptied before it fills. A full trap smells, drains slowly and pushes grease into the line beyond it. Cleaning removes the trapped fat and solids and lets the trap work again.

Commercial kitchen drains clog faster than residential ones because of the volume of fat, starch and food waste. A cable clears the blockage in the moment, which is the job of drain cleaning. When buildup coats the pipe wall, hydro jetting is the better tool, so ask about it when you book.

Backflow testing: planned work with specific requirements

A backflow preventer is a valve assembly that stops used water from flowing back into the drinking supply. Many commercial buildings with irrigation, fire lines or boilers have one, and the assembly is tested on a set schedule. This is planned work, so book it in advance rather than waiting for a notice to expire.

Testing must be done by a licensed plumber with the required backflow certification. We dispatch one for that work and do not send a general plumber to certify an assembly. If an assembly fails the test, repair or replacement is a separate job with its own price.

Storm drain and area drain cleaning

Roof drains, area drains and catch basins fill with leaves, silt and trash, and a blocked drain sends rain into doorways and parking areas. Cleaning clears the grate and the line below it, and a camera shows whether the pipe is broken or just clogged.

Parts of Coronado sit low near the bay, so it makes sense to clear storm drains before the rain forecast, not after the lot is already wet. Ask your property manager who maintains those drains, since many are shared among tenants.
