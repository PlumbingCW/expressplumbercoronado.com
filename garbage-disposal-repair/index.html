A disposal that leaks under the sink or sends water back up the basin needs fast help, and our garbage disposal repair line in Coronado, CA answers 24/7. A disposal is a motor with a grinding plate under the sink drain, and it only works well with cold water running and the right waste going in. While you wait, switch it off at the wall and keep your hand out of the drain.

Most disposal problems are one of three things. The unit is jammed, the motor has burned out, or a seal or connection is leaking. Each has a different fix, and some are not worth repairing. We sort out which it is, quote the work first, then either repair or replace the unit. A same-day appointment is booked when one is available.

Disposal leaking under the sink or flooding the cabinet

A disposal sits directly under a running sink, so a leak can fill the cabinet each time someone rinses a plate. Stopping water use ends most of the damage before the plumber arrives.

A leak at midnight gets the same attention as one at noon, and same-day visits are open when available.

What to do before you touch a jammed disposal

Turn the disposal off at the wall switch, then unplug it or switch off its breaker. Never put a hand into the opening, even when the unit is off. Use tongs or pliers and a flashlight to lift out anything visible.

Most disposals have a hex socket in the center of the underside so the motor can be turned by hand once the power is off. Skip chemical drain cleaners, which can splash back and attack seals. If the unit hums but does not spin or leaks from the body, stop and call.

Disposal repair and replacement, step by step

A jam is the easiest repair. With the power off, a hex key in the bottom socket turns the plate back and forth to free the obstruction, then the red reset button restarts the motor. If it hums again right away, something is still caught or the motor is failing, and the unit comes out for a closer look.

Replacement starts when the housing leaks or the motor dies. We disconnect the drain and dishwasher line, remove the mounting ring, set the new unit and reconnect the electrical supply. Horsepower, feed type and sink depth are chosen to suit your household, and the dishwasher inlet knockout is opened if one is needed.

When a repair would cost nearly as much as a replacement, we say so. A motor that has overheated twice is a unit at the end of its life, and spending more on it only delays the next call.

What to keep out of a disposal

Most jams and many clogs begin with something that never should have gone down the drain.

  • ✓Grease, oil and fat, which cool and coat the pipe walls further down the line.
  • ✓Coffee grounds, eggshells and starchy foods such as pasta and rice, which pack into a paste.
  • ✓Fibrous scraps such as celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves, which wrap around the plate.
  • ✓Bones, fruit pits and shells, which are too hard for a small motor.
  • ✓Non-food items like twist ties, bottle caps, glass and cleaning pads.
  • ✓Harsh chemical drain openers, which can damage seals and are hard on skin if they splash back.
Coronado local note

What goes down your Coronado kitchen drain

Coronado's sewer system depends on 17 pump stations and over 45 miles of underground pipe, run by the city's wastewater division. Whatever you grind or rinse away ends up in that system, and grease and fibrous waste are the things that cause clogs and trouble for pumps.

The lateral from your house to the city main, up to and including the tie-in at the main, belongs to the property owner. A lateral blocked by years of grease is your repair, so keeping fats out of the disposal protects your own line. If one has built up, sewer line cleaning clears it.

When the sink clogs even though the disposal runs

A motor that spins but a basin that will not empty means the blockage sits past the disposal, in the trap or the drain line. Grease, food paste and soap film narrow the pipe a little more each week.

That is drain cleaning work, and the plumber can tell by running water through the line. If the pipe has heavy buildup, hydro jetting scours it more thoroughly than a cable. The same advice applies to homes in Chula Vista, where we also dispatch.

Repair the unit or put in a new one

Repair if

  • A jam is the only problem and the motor is strong.
  • The leak is at the flange or a pipe connection.
  • The unit is not old and has not had repeated failures.
  • Parts such as gaskets and mounts are cheap and in stock.

Replace if

  • The body is cracked or rusted through.
  • The motor has failed or tripped the reset repeatedly.
  • The grinding plate is badly pitted and jams on soft food.
  • A repair would cost close to a new unit.
